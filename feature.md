# Feature Specification: Home Page

The home page is designed with high-conversion D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) principles, targeting a modern, premium, and trustworthy experience.

## 1. Navigation & Header
* **Sticky Announcement Bar:** Displays ongoing promotions (e.g., "Free Shipping & 100 Night Trial") with a smooth transition.
* **Mega Menu:** Hover-triggered category navigation with product imagery and quick links.
* **Utility Icons:** Search bar (with predictive results), User Profile, and Slide-out Mini-Cart with a free-shipping progress bar.

## 2. Hero & Trust Elements
* **Cinematic Hero Banner:** Full-width high-quality lifestyle imagery with dark-theme or high-contrast overlay text and dual CTAs ("Shop Now" & "Take the Quiz").
* **Trust & Features Banner:** Three key value propositions (100-Night Trial, 15-Year Warranty, Free Delivery) with elegant, minimalist icons.
* **Social Proof & Media Badges:** Banner displaying star rating (e.g., 4.8/5) and features in recognized design or sleep publications.

## 3. Core Product Modules
* **Interactive Material Layer Section:** A visually engaging scroll-driven explainer showing the internal layers of the product (e.g., Memory foam, cooling gel, base support).
* **Featured Collections Tab:** Toggle between Bestsellers, New Arrivals, and Accessories without a page refresh.

## 4. Trust and Retention Modules
* **Testimonials Carousel:** Display customer reviews with high-quality user-generated content and "verified buyer" tags.
* **FAQ Accordion:** Expandable answers to common pre-purchase queries (delivery, unboxing, trials).
